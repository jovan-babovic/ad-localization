# PropFusion (example company)

Sample setup for `/localize example`. `/setup` writes your own version of this file to `config/company.md`.
The example ads are demo ads made for this repo, not live campaigns.

## Company and site
- Company: PropFusion. Website: https://www.propfusion.com
- Product in these ads: Field Mode by PropFusion, the inspection app built for reserve study professionals.
- Demo page: https://www.propfusion.com/demo

## Who buys (ICP)
Reserve study professionals: the specialists and engineers who walk a community association's property, inspect
its common components (roofs, paving, pools, mailboxes) and write the reserve study and funding plan.

## Ads and channels
- Google search ads (headlines and descriptions).
- Meta feed ads (square image) and LinkedIn single image ads.
- A 30-second product video with a voice-over and music.

## Voice
Plain, practical and calm. Talks like a colleague who has done the fieldwork. Short sentences, no hype words.

## Claims that need care
Every claim in the ads comes from PropFusion's own pages; none is an independent result.
- Each photo and note links to the right component as you capture it, on site.
  ([Field Mode update](https://www.propfusion.com/update/field-mode))
- Back at the desk, the data is already organized and ready for the report. (same page)
- Geotagged photos; voice notes transcribed by AI with audio and text kept; sketch on the photo. (same page,
  and [For Reserve Specialists](https://www.propfusion.com/reserve-pro))
- "No more mystery photos", "no guessing later", "no double-entry": promises about how the data is organized,
  never stronger than the English.
- Never add a speed, time-saved or accuracy number. The site's "5x faster" and "-60% rework" figures describe the
  whole product, not Field Mode, and are not used in these ads.
- Don't mention app stores or show store badges.

## Notes for translators
- "Reserve study" is a North American term. Other markets may use a different legal concept; keep the meaning
  (a long-term plan for repair and replacement costs) and flag the term for a local expert.
- "Component" is a building element tracked in the study (a roof, a boiler), not a software component.
- The app's screens stay in English in the video: the app ships in English.
- propfusion.com has no language versions, so the demo link stays propfusion.com/demo.

Source: propfusion.com pages linked above, checked 2026-10-14.
